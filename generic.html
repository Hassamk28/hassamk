About Me

Data analyst who likes building things people actually use.

Hi, I'm Hassam. I came to data analytics from the retail industry, where I worked for much of my life. I earned the Google Data Analytics certificate and have been building my skills in SQL, Python, Tableau, Power BI, and Excel ever since.

Today I work at Meals on Wheels Collin County, a nonprofit that delivers meals and wellness checks to older and disabled neighbors. There I build the data tools and reports the team relies on. One example is a volunteer check-in and route-assignment system that staff and volunteers use on every delivery day. I also clean up messy exports from our systems and turn them into reports leadership can trust.

Before that, as a data entry manager at a small business, I used Python to scrape and organize data, used Excel every day to keep records accurate, and picked up HTML and CSS along the way.

What I care about most is making data simple, accurate, and useful for the people who need it. That could be a board member reading a one-page summary or a volunteer checking in from a car line.
